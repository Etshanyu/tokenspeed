# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Token Speed welcomes participation from people of every background and experience level. We are committed to a harassment-free, respectful and constructive project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific in technical discussion.
- Give useful feedback and assume good faith while evaluating evidence critically.
- Respect privacy, different environments and differing levels of experience.
- Never publish another person's logs, credentials or identifying information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption, doxxing and unwanted sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. They may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe and productive. Decisions should be proportionate, consistent and documented without exposing private reporter information.

This policy applies to project spaces and to public representation of the project.
